Frequently asked

Why does the turret shoot where I'm going, not where I am?

That is the game. TELL learns your movement habits — on your device only — and aims at its best guess of your next move. The translucent red ghost is that guess. If the ghost keeps landing on you, you have become predictable.

What is READ%?

How often the model's short-term predictions about you have been right recently. Higher means it has your number.

The game got harder over a few days. Is that a bug?

No — the model remembers you between runs. It is not the game getting harder; it is the game knowing you better. "Wipe its memory" on the results screen (paid with coins earned in play) resets everything it has learned.

Does TELL collect any data?

No. The model is a small table of numbers stored on your device. Nothing leaves your phone: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no network access at all. See the privacy policy.
